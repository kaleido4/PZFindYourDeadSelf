description=A red skull with your character's name marks where you died on the world map.
description=[list]
description=[*]If your body reanimates, the skull follows your zombie while it is in a loaded area, and stays at its last known position while it is not.
description=[*]When your zombie is killed, the skull stays where it fell.
description=[*]The skull disappears when the game would remove the corpse (Hours for Corpse Removal sandbox option). Note that the game keeps player corpses forever unless the server option "Remove Player Corpses On Corpse Removal" is on; corpses of killed zombies are removed normally.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Extra feature[/h2]
description=In Vanilla, turning on god mode while already dying shows the death screen but leaves no corpse and no zombie behind, so all your gear is gone. This mod makes sure every death leaves a corpse that can still reanimate as usual.
description=

description=[list]

description=[/list]
